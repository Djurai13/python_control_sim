# PPCIS v2.0 Master Plan

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# Executive Summary

PPCIS v2.0 is the next-generation evolution of the Python Population Control Intelligence System (PPCIS).

The objective of PPCIS v2.0 is to transform PPCIS from a simulation-focused prototype into a production-ready ecological intelligence platform capable of supporting invasive species management, ecological monitoring, forecasting, spatial intelligence, operational planning, and decision support.

The program will preserve the strategic assets developed in PPCIS v1.0 while progressively introducing enterprise-grade architecture, data persistence, analytics, GIS intelligence, operational workflows, and reporting capabilities.

---

# Program Mission

Transform PPCIS v1.0 into a production-ready ecological intelligence platform while preserving existing intellectual property, simulation capabilities, forecasting capabilities, ecological control logic, and domain knowledge.

---

# Program Vision

PPCIS will become a unified platform capable of:

* Managing ecological observations
* Managing removal activities
* Monitoring invasive species populations
* Supporting simulation and forecasting
* Delivering GIS intelligence
* Supporting operational decision making
* Producing management reports
* Providing long-term historical analysis

# Core Architectural Principles

## Preserve Existing Value

Existing intellectual property should be extended rather than replaced.

## Progressive Evolution

PPCIS v2.0 will evolve incrementally from PPCIS v1.0.

## Design Before Implementation

Architecture and design decisions must be completed before implementation begins.

## One Sprint One Objective

Each sprint must have a clearly defined objective and deliverable.

## Documentation Driven Development

Documentation is treated as a first-class deliverable.

# Target Platform Architecture

PPCIS v2.0 consists of seven major platforms.

## Platform 1 — Data Import Platform

Purpose:

Provide ingestion of ecological and operational data.

Capabilities:

* CSV Import
* Excel Import
* GeoJSON Import
* Validation
* Quality Scoring
* Import Auditing

---

## Platform 2 — Database Platform

Purpose:

Provide persistent storage for all PPCIS data.

Technology:

* PostgreSQL
* PostGIS

Capabilities:

* Data Persistence
* Spatial Data Management
* Historical Data Storage
* Audit Storage

---

## Platform 3 — Simulation Platform

Purpose:

Execute ecological population simulations.

Capabilities:

* Population Simulation
* Scenario Analysis
* Control Strategy Evaluation
* Historical Simulation Tracking

---

## Platform 4 — Forecast Intelligence Platform

Purpose:

Predict future ecological conditions.

Capabilities:

* Forecast Models
* Trend Analysis
* Scenario Forecasting
* Forecast History

---

## Platform 5 — GIS Intelligence Platform

Purpose:

Provide spatial analysis and visualization.

Capabilities:

* Spatial Queries
* Hotspot Detection
* Coverage Analysis
* Risk Mapping
* Operational Mapping

---

## Platform 6 — Operations Platform

Purpose:

Manage field operations and intervention activities.

Capabilities:

* Task Management
* Assignments
* Alert Management
* Operational Reporting

---

## Platform 7 — Reporting Platform

Purpose:

Provide management and executive reporting.

Capabilities:

* PDF Reports
* CSV Exports
* Executive Dashboards
* Scheduled Reports

# Success Criteria

PPCIS v2.0 will be considered successful when:

* Real-world ecological data can be imported
* Historical simulation data is persistent
* Forecasts are auditable
* GIS intelligence is operational
* Operational workflows are managed
* Reporting is available
* Security is enforced
* Platform deployment is repeatable
* Platform maintenance is sustainable
